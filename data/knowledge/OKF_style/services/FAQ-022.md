# What is the process for seminar submission?

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

**Q:** What is the process for seminar submission?

**A:** Get your topic approved 3 weeks ahead, upload slides and a 2-page abstract 48 hours before the talk, then present for 15 minutes plus 5 minutes of questions.
