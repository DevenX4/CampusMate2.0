# How do I submit my capstone project?

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

**Q:** How do I submit my capstone project?

**A:** Submit the report (PDF), repository link and 5-minute demo video through the demo project portal by 17:00 on the deadline in the academic calendar. Late submissions lose 10% per day for up to 3 days.
