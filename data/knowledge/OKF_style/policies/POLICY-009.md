Booking rules for the seminar hall, discussion rooms and GPU facility.

- CM-SYN-POL-009 §1. The IT Seminar Hall (FCL-IT-001) must be booked at least 5 working days in advance through the Department Office.

- CM-SYN-POL-009 §2. Discussion rooms (FCL-IT-005, FCL-IT-006) can be booked for a maximum of 2 hours per team per day.

- CM-SYN-POL-009 §3. GPU Computing Facility (FCL-IT-008) access requires a faculty-approved project and is allocated in 24-hour job quotas.

- CM-SYN-POL-009 §4. Bookings not used within 15 minutes of the start time are released.
