# CM-SYN-POL-005 Project Evaluation Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

Panel-based evaluation of capstone and major projects.

- CM-SYN-POL-005 §1. Every project is evaluated by a panel of at least two faculty members plus the guide.

- CM-SYN-POL-005 §2. Evaluation happens in two stages: a mid-review and a final presentation in the IT Seminar Hall (FCL-IT-001).

- CM-SYN-POL-005 §3. Demo rubric weights: problem definition 15%, design 20%, implementation 30%, testing 15%, report and presentation 20%.

- CM-SYN-POL-005 §4. Panel scores are finalised within 5 working days and published on the notice board.

- CM-SYN-POL-005 §5. Students may request a re-review within 3 working days of publication.
