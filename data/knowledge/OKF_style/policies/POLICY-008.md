# CM-SYN-POL-008 Faculty Consultation Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

How students meet faculty outside class.

- CM-SYN-POL-008 §1. Every faculty member publishes weekly consultation hours in the Faculty Directory (DOC-FACULTY-001).

- CM-SYN-POL-008 §2. Students book a slot by emailing the faculty member at least 24 hours in advance.

- CM-SYN-POL-008 §3. Consultations take place in the faculty cabin or in Discussion Room 1/2 (FCL-IT-005, FCL-IT-006).

- CM-SYN-POL-008 §4. Urgent academic issues can be raised at the Department Office & Student Help Desk (FCL-IT-010).
