# CM-SYN-POL-003 Laboratory Safety Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

Electrical, hardware and data-safety rules for laboratories and maker spaces.

- CM-SYN-POL-003 §1. Food and drinks are not permitted inside any laboratory.

- CM-SYN-POL-003 §2. Hardware in LAB-IT-007 (IoT & Robotics Lab) and FCL-IT-003 (Innovation & Incubation Centre) may be operated only after a safety induction.

- CM-SYN-POL-003 §3. Report damaged cables, overheating equipment or data breaches immediately to the responsible faculty member.

- CM-SYN-POL-003 §4. Emergency exits and fire extinguishers must never be blocked.

- CM-SYN-POL-003 §5. Students must not connect unknown USB devices to the isolated security test network in LAB-IT-005.
