# CM-SYN-POL-002 Laboratory Usage Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

Rules for using any of the ten IT department laboratories LAB-IT-001 to LAB-IT-010.

- CM-SYN-POL-002 §1. Students may use a laboratory only during scheduled practicals or after registering a lab slot with the responsible faculty member of that lab.

- CM-SYN-POL-002 §2. Lab slot registration opens every Monday at 09:00 through the department office email it-labs@campusmate-demo.example.

- CM-SYN-POL-002 §3. Personal software may not be installed on lab workstations; requests go to the lab's responsible faculty.

- CM-SYN-POL-002 §4. Lab computers must be logged off and seats left clean at the end of each session.

- CM-SYN-POL-002 §5. Repeated violations are reported to the Head of Department.
