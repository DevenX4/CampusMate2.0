# CM-SYN-POL-004 Capstone Project Submission Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

How B.Tech IT Capstone Project-I, II and III deliverables are submitted.

- CM-SYN-POL-004 §1. Teams of 2–4 students register a project title with the Project Coordinator (FAC-IT-020) by the topic deadline in the academic calendar.

- CM-SYN-POL-004 §2. Each submission must include a report (PDF), source-code repository link, and a 5-minute demo video.

- CM-SYN-POL-004 §3. Final submissions are made through the demo project portal by 17:00 on the deadline date; late submissions lose 10% per day for up to 3 days.

- CM-SYN-POL-004 §4. Similarity above 20% in the report requires a written explanation to the Project Coordinator.

- CM-SYN-POL-004 §5. Printed copies are not required unless requested by the evaluation panel.
