# CM-SYN-POL-001 Attendance Policy (Demo)

> SYNTHETIC DATA — for CampusMate development and testing only. Not an official Ganpat University fact.

_This is a synthetic policy created for CampusMate testing. It is not a Ganpat University regulation._

Sets the demo minimum attendance expectation for IT courses.

- CM-SYN-POL-001 §1. Students are expected to attend at least 75% of lecture and 80% of laboratory sessions in each course (synthetic threshold).

- CM-SYN-POL-001 §2. Attendance is reviewed at mid-semester (see calendar CAL-2026-27-006) and before the end-semester examinations.

- CM-SYN-POL-001 §3. Students below threshold receive a written advisory from the course faculty and may be asked to meet the Head of Department (FAC-IT-001).

- CM-SYN-POL-001 §4. Medical or institutional-duty absences must be reported to the Department Office (FCL-IT-010) within 7 days.
