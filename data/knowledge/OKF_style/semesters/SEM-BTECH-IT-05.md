# BTECH IT Semester V

**BTECH IT Semester V** (SEM-BTECH-IT-05): total credits 23.0 (TH 15.0, PR 8.0), contact hours 31.0, total marks 850.

Regular courses: 2CEIT501 COMPUTER ARCHITECTURE & ORGANIZATION; 2CEIT502 SOFTWARE ENGINEERING; 2CEIT503 COMPUTER NETWORKS; 2CEIT504 CAPSTONE PROJECT-I; 2HS5101 APTITUDE SKILL BUILDING-I.

ELECTIVE-I (EGRP-BTECH-IT-05-ELECTIVE-I, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT5PE1 ADVANCED JAVA; 2CEIT5PE2 COMPUTER GRAPHICS & VISUALIZATION; 2CEIT5PE3 QUANTUM COMPUTING; 2CEIT5PE4 SOFTWARE PACKAGES.

ELECTIVE-II (EGRP-BTECH-IT-05-ELECTIVE-II, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT5PE5 MOBILE APPLICATION DEVELOPMENT; 2CEIT5PE6 HUMAN COMPUTER INTERACTION; 2CEIT5PE7 INNOVATION & ENTREPRENEURSHIP; 2CEIT5PE8 ADVANCED PROGRAMMING WITH .NET.
