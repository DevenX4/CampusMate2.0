# BTECH IT Semester II

**BTECH IT Semester II** (SEM-BTECH-IT-02): total credits 20.0 (TH 12.0, PR 8.0), contact hours 30.0, total marks 750.

Regular courses: 2BS102 MATHEMATICS - II; 2BS103 PHYSICS; 2HS101 COMMUNICATION SKILLS; 2ES102 WORKSHOP MANUFACTURING PRACTICE; 2ES104 PROGRAMMING FOR PROBLEM SOLVING; 2ES111 IT WORKSHOP.

UHV_ELECTIVE-I (EGRP-BTECH-IT-02-UHV-ELECTIVE-I, 2.0 credits): choose one of UHV1IPDC1 UNIVERSAL HUMAN VALUE (INTEGRATED PERSONALITY DEVELOPMENT COURSE-1); UHV3DYSBG1 UNIVERSAL HUMAN VALUE (DISCOVER YOURSELF THROUGH BHAGAVAD GITA-1).

AUDIT (EGRP-BTECH-IT-02-AUDIT, 0.0 credits): choose one of 2AC103 ENVIRONMENTAL STUDIES AND DISASTER MANAGEMENT.
