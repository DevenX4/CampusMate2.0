# MTECH IT Semester III

**MTECH IT Semester III** (SEM-MTECH-IT-03): total credits 18.0 (TH 6.0, PR 12.0), contact hours 30.0, total marks 600.

Regular courses: 3CEIT301 MAJOR PROJECT PART - I; 3CEIT302 SEMINAR.

ELECTIVE-V (EGRP-MTECH-IT-03-ELECTIVE-V, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT303 SEMANTIC WEB AND SOCIAL NETWORKS; 3CEIT304 DATA COMPRESSION; 3CEIT305 INTERNET OF THINGS; 3CEIT306 BIG DATA ANALYTICS.

ELECTIVE-VI (EGRP-MTECH-IT-03-ELECTIVE-VI, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT307 NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING; 3CEIT308 COMPILER DESIGN; 3CEIT309 DEEP LEARNING; 3CEIT310 CYBER SECURITY.
