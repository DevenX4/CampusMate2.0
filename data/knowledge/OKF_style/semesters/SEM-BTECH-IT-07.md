# BTECH IT Semester VII

**BTECH IT Semester VII** (SEM-BTECH-IT-07): total credits 23.0 (TH 12.0, PR 11.0), contact hours 34.0, total marks 750.

Regular courses: 2CEIT701 COMPILER DESIGN; 2CEIT702 BIG DATA ANALYTICS; 2CEIT703 CAPSTONE PROJECT-III; 2CEIT704 SOFT SKILLS.

ELECTIVE-V (EGRP-BTECH-IT-07-ELECTIVE-V, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT78PE1 DEEP LEARNING; 2CEIT78PE2 BLOCKCHAIN TECHNOLOGIES; 2CEIT78PE3 FUNDAMENTALS OF IMAGE PROCESSING; 2CEIT78PE4 MOBILE COMPUTING; 2CEIT78PE5 SEARCH ENGINE OPTIMIZATION; 2CEIT78PE6 VIRTUAL REALITY.

ELECTIVE-VI (EGRP-BTECH-IT-07-ELECTIVE-VI, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT78PE7 NATURAL LANGUAGE PROCESSING; 2CEIT78PE8 FORENSICS & CYBER LAW; 2CEIT78PE9 ADVANCED ALGORITHMS; 2CEIT78PE10 FOG COMPUTING; 2CEIT78PE11 FAULT TOLERANT SYSTEMS; 2CEIT78PE12 HIGH PERFORMANCE COMPUTING; 2CEIT78PE13 COMPUTER VISION.
