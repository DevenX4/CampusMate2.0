# MTECH IT Semester I

**MTECH IT Semester I** (SEM-MTECH-IT-01): total credits 21.0 (TH 15.0, PR 6.0), contact hours 27.0, total marks 800.

Regular courses: 3CEIT101 COMPUTATIONAL INTELLIGENCE; 3CEIT102 COMPUTER ALGORITHMS; 3CEIT103 CRYPTOGRAPHY AND NETWORK SECURITY; 3CEIT104 RESEARCH ORIENTATION.

ELECTIVE-I (EGRP-MTECH-IT-01-ELECTIVE-I, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT105 WIRELESS NETWORKS; 3CEIT106 ADVANCED TOPICS IN NETWORKS; 3CEIT107 INTERNET TECHNOLOGIES; 3CEIT108 NETWORK PROGRAMMING AND MANAGEMENT; 3CEIT109 HIGH PERFORMANCE COMPUTING.

ELECTIVE-II (EGRP-MTECH-IT-01-ELECTIVE-II, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT110 DISTRIBUTED COMPUTING; 3CEIT111 SERVICE ORIENTED COMPUTING; 3CEIT112 ADVANCE SOFTWARE ENGINEERING; 3CEIT113 REAL TIME SYSTEMS.
