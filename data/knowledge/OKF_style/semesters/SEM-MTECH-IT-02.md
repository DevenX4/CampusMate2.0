# MTECH IT Semester II

**MTECH IT Semester II** (SEM-MTECH-IT-02): total credits 23.0 (TH 18.0, PR 5.0), contact hours 28.0, total marks 850.

Regular courses: 3CEIT201 DIGITAL IMAGE PROCESSING; 3CEIT202 DATA MINING AND DATA WAREHOUSING; 3CEIT203 CLOUD COMPUTING.

ELECTIVE-III (EGRP-MTECH-IT-02-ELECTIVE-III, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT204 ADVANCED OPERATING SYSTEM; 3CEIT205 ADVANCED COMPUTER ARCHITECTURE; 3CEIT206 SOFTWARE ARCHITECTURE AND DESIGN PATTERN; 3CEIT207 PUBLIC KEY INFRASTRUCTURE.

ELECTIVE-IV (EGRP-MTECH-IT-02-ELECTIVE-IV, 4.0 credits): choose one of 3CEIT208 SATELLITE NETWORKS; 3CEIT209 ESSENTIAL MATHEMATICS; 3CEIT210 MODELING AND SIMULATION; 3CEIT211 ADVANCED DATABASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM.

OPEN (EGRP-MTECH-IT-02-OPEN, 3.0 credits): choose one of 3OS201 OPEN SUBJECT.
