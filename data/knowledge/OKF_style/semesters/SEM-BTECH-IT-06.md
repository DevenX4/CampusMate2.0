# BTECH IT Semester VI

**BTECH IT Semester VI** (SEM-BTECH-IT-06): total credits 23.0 (TH 15.0, PR 8.0), contact hours 31.0, total marks 900.

Regular courses: 2CEIT601 THEORY OF COMPUTATION; 2CEIT602 ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE; 2CEIT603 CLOUD COMPUTING; 2CEIT604 USER EXPERIENCE DESIGN; 2CEIT605 CAPSTONE PROJECT-II; 2HS6101 APTITUDE SKILL BUILDING–II.

ELECTIVE-III (EGRP-BTECH-IT-06-ELECTIVE-III, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT6PE1 WEB TECHNOLOGY; 2CEIT6PE2 SOFTWARE VALIDATION & TESTING; 2CEIT6PE3 CRYPTOGRAPHY AND NETWORK SECURITY; 2CEIT6PE4 DATA SCIENCES AND MACHINE LEARNING; 2CEIT6PE5 GAME DESIGN & DEVELOPMENT.

ELECTIVE-IV (EGRP-BTECH-IT-06-ELECTIVE-IV, 4.0 credits): choose one of 2CEIT6PE6 ROBOTICS; 2CEIT6PE7 ETHICAL HACKING; 2CEIT6PE8 IT INFRASTRUCTURE & MANAGEMENT; 2CEIT6PE9 INTERNET OF THINGS.
