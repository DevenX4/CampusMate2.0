# BTECH IT Semester III

**BTECH IT Semester III** (SEM-BTECH-IT-03): total credits 24.0 (TH 18.0, PR 6.0), contact hours 32.0, total marks 800.

Regular courses: 2BS3102 DISCRETE MATHEMATICS & PROBABILITY; 2CEIT301 DIGITAL ELECTRONICS; 2CEIT302 OBJECT ORIENTED PROGRAMMING; 2CEIT303 DATABASE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM; 2CEIT304 DATA STRUCTURES.

AUDIT (EGRP-BTECH-IT-03-AUDIT, 0.0 credits): choose one of 2AC301 AUDIT COURSE.

UHV_ELECTIVE-II (EGRP-BTECH-IT-03-UHV-ELECTIVE-II, 2.0 credits): choose one of UHV2IPDC2 UNIVERSAL HUMAN VALUE (INTEGRATED PERSONALITY DEVELOPMENT COURSE-2); UHV4DYSBG2 UNIVERSAL HUMAN VALUE (DISCOVER YOURSELF THROUGH BHAGAVAD GITA-2).
